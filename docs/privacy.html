Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What the app collects

Purchases. If you buy Estimate Maker Pro, Apple processes the payment, and RevenueCat, the service the app uses to confirm Pro, keeps your purchase history for this app under a random anonymous identifier. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

That is all. It is the only item on the app's App Store privacy label: Purchases, not linked to you.

What stays on your iPhone

Your estimates, quotes, invoices, receipts, clients, saved items, business details, logo and signatures are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, and nothing you type is uploaded. A PDF leaves your phone only when you share, mail or print it.
