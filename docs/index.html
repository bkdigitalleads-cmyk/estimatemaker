Estimate Maker

Estimates, invoices and receipts that look like your business.

For contractors, freelancers and small shops. Price the job on site, get it signed on your phone, send the invoice when it is done, and the receipt when it is paid.

Get it on the App Store

iPhone. Free to download; your first two PDFs are free.

What it does
EstEstimates and quotes with your logo, address and license number, line items with quantity, unit and rate, discount, tax and a deposit.
InvOne tap turns an accepted estimate into an invoice with every line; one more turns a paid invoice into a receipt.
SignYour saved signature on every page, and Sign here for your client on the spot.
PDFThree clean templates, Ledger, Banner and Plain, in US Letter or A4, sent with the share sheet, Mail or Print.
FindSearch by client, number or amount; filter by type and status. The month's billed total and what is unpaid, on the first screen.
OwnEach type numbers on its own, with your own prefixes. Your currency and number format, from your iPhone.
The PDFs

Every document prints your own business details. The samples here use a made-up carpentry business.

Private

No account and no server. Your documents, clients and items live on your iPhone, and a PDF leaves it only when you send it. See the privacy policy.
