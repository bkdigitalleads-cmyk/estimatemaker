Support

Estimate Maker is on the App Store as Estimate Maker & Receipt Maker. Here is how it works.

Contact

Email bkdigitalleads@gmail.com and we reply within two business days. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have.

Your business details

Settings, Your business: your name, logo (from Photos), address, phone, email, website and a tax or license number if you have one. They print at the top of every document. A PDF does not go out until the business has a name.

Make an estimate, quote, invoice or receipt

Tap New document and pick the type. Pick or add a client, set the date and the due or valid-until date, and add lines: a description, quantity, unit and rate. Tap Saved items to add your usual work and parts in one tap, or turn on Save to my items when you add a line. Discount (a percent or an amount), tax, a deposit or what was paid, notes and terms are on the same page. Everything saves as you go.

Estimate to invoice to receipt

On an estimate or quote, tap Make invoice: a new invoice with every line, its own number and today's date, and the estimate is marked accepted. On an invoice, tap Make receipt: a receipt for the full amount, and the invoice is marked paid. Duplicate is under the three dots.

Signatures

Settings, Your signature: draw it once and it prints on the business line. On an estimate, tap Sign here and hand your phone to the client; their signature, name and the date print on the Accepted by line.

Send a PDF

Tap Send to see the PDF as it will print. Pick Ledger, Banner or Plain and a color, then Share PDF, Mail (with the client's email filled in) or Print. Paper size (US Letter or A4), currency, tax and document numbers are in Settings.

Pro and restoring

Drafts, clients and saved items are free with no limit, and your first two PDFs go out free. After that, sending a new PDF needs Pro: weekly with a free trial for new subscribers, yearly, or lifetime with one payment. Prices are shown in the app. Documents you made stay yours to open and edit either way, and a PDF that went out free can always go out again. On a new iPhone, tap Restore purchases in Settings. Manage or cancel a subscription in the Settings app, your name, Subscriptions.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your documents live on your iPhone, so an iCloud or computer backup of the phone carries them over. There is no account to sign in to.

Privacy

Nothing you type is uploaded. See the privacy policy.
